About Code Genesis

Code Genesis is a global software development company delivering scalable digital solutions across web, mobile, cloud, AI/ML, and DevOps. We work with startups and enterprises worldwide to build reliable, high-impact technology products while fostering a culture of learning, integrity, and innovation

Role Overview

We are looking for motivated Fresh Graduates or Final-Year Students who are passionate about software development and eager to start their professional careers in a real-world engineering environment.

This role is ideal for individuals who have strong fundamentals, a willingness to learn, and a collaborative mindset. You will work under the guidance of experienced engineers and gain exposure to production-grade systems and modern development practices.

Key Responsibilities

Assist in designing, developing, testing, and maintaining software applications
Write clean, readable, and well-documented code under supervision
Collaborate with senior developers, designers, and QA teams
Participate in code reviews and team discussions
Debug issues and contribute to continuous improvements
Learn and apply best practices in software engineering, security, and performance
Support ongoing projects across web, mobile, cloud, or backend systems
Required Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree completed or in final year in Computer Science, Software Engineering, IT, or a related field
Understanding of at least one programming language (e.g., Python, JavaScript, Java, C#, or similar)
Basic knowledge of data structures, algorithms, and OOP concepts
Familiarity with web technologies, databases, or APIs is a plus
Willingness to learn new tools, frameworks, and technologies
Good communication and teamwork skills
Nice to Have (Not Mandatory)

Academic projects, internships, or personal projects
Exposure to frameworks such as React, Node.js, Django, Flask, or similar
Basic understanding of Git/version control
Interest in cloud platforms, DevOps, or AI/ML
What We Offer

Structured onboarding and mentorship
Real-world project exposure with global clients
Continuous learning and skill-development opportunities
Collaborative and inclusive work culture
Career growth path based on performance and learning
Competitive compensation aligned with entry-level market standards
